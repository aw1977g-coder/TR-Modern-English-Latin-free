# Why You Were Never Taught This: 60% of English is French/Latin

In school they told you English is English. They didn't tell you 60% of it is French and Latin forced on us in 1066.

## The Two Englishes

### 1. The Real English - 40% Germanic (450 AD) - OUR WORDS
This is Old English from the Angles, Saxons, and Jutes - tribes from North Germany / Denmark / Netherlands who sailed to Britain.

These are the words you use 90% of the day:
I, you, he, we, man, wife, child, kin, folk, house, home, earth, heaven, water, fire, bread, book, word, god, holy, love, hate, live, die, father, mother, son, daughter, shield, sword, king, queen

This is the BONE of English.

### 2. The Invader English - 60% Latin + French (1066 AD) - THEIR WORDS
In 1066 William the Conqueror from France invaded England. For 300 years, kings spoke French. They made the laws and church in French/Latin.

They replaced our words:

## Why This Matters for the Bible

The Bible was first written in Hebrew and Greek - simple picture words: dip, gather, turn, breath.

When it came to English, translators used 60% Latin church words to sound fancy: virgin, spirit, prophet, salvation.

We are putting it BACK to the 40% - the simple Germanic words that match the Greek better.

Greek *baptizo* = dip (Germanic) NOT baptism (Latin)
Greek *pneuma* = breath (Germanic) NOT spirit (Latin)
Greek *prophetes* = forth-teller (Germanic) NOT prophet (Greek via Latin)

Germanic = closer to Greek than Latin is.

## Where Does Germanic English Come From?

Not from modern Germany. From the tribes:
- Angles - from Angeln, Germany/Denmark border
- Saxons - from Saxony, Germany
- Jutes - from Jutland, Denmark

They spoke Old English. Modern German is their cousin, not their father.

English is Germanic at its root, with a French coat of paint. We are stripping the paint off.

## Why They Don't Teach This

Because if you know 60% of English is invader words, you start asking why you need them. Schools teach the 60% as "proper" English.

This project proves you can write a whole Bible with only the 40% and it reads cleaner.
